# Privacy Policy

_Updated 12 November 2026_

TabPillow is built around a simple rule: the extension works locally and collects no personal data.
This page explains what that means for the extension and the website.

## Extension: no data collection

TabPillow does not collect, transmit or sell browsing history, page content, URLs or usage
statistics. Settings, statistics and saved workspaces are stored locally with `chrome.storage` and
do not leave your device. Settings may sync between your devices using Chrome sync if you enable it.

## Permissions

The extension requests `tabs` (manage and suspend tabs), `storage` (local settings and workspaces),
`alarms` (sleep timers), `system.memory` (free RAM), `notifications` (one low-memory alert) and
`contextMenus` (keep-awake menu item). There are no host permissions, no content scripts and no
remote code. See [permissions.md](permissions.md).

## Website analytics

gettabpillow.fun uses Umami Cloud, a cookie-less analytics service. It records aggregated page views
and install-button clicks without collecting personal data, building profiles or tracking you across
sites. This applies to the website only; the extension sends no telemetry.

## Third parties

We do not sell or share data. The website is hosted by Hostinger and its CDN may process technical
request logs to deliver pages and protect the site. The Chrome Web Store is operated by Google under
its own policies.

## Deleting data

Remove the extension in `chrome://extensions` and Chrome deletes its local data. Statistics can be
reset in the extension settings. Website analytics is aggregated and cannot be tied back to an
individual visitor.
